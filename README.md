# Medical AI Research & Evaluation Portfolio

Hello! I am Brenda Depazi, a physiotherapy undergraduate student at Alupe University, passionate about bridging clinical healthcare and artificial intelligence. 

This repository showcases my work in auditing AI-generated medical text, designing clinical evaluation frameworks, and synthesizing research on AI in musculoskeletal care.

---

## 📂 Repository Contents

* **[Clinical AI Evaluation Rubric](./eval_frameworks/clinical_benchmark_rubric.md)**: A structured scoring framework used to grade AI medical accuracy, safety, and clarity.
* **[Clinical Audits & Hallucination Corrections](./clinical_audits/)**: Side-by-side case studies catching and correcting dangerous AI medical outputs (lower back pain & tennis elbow).
* **[Literature Synthesis](./literature_reviews/ai_in_rehab_synthesis.md)**: A research review examining the limitations of AI in physical triage and rehabilitation.
